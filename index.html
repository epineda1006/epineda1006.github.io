Research Statement

Capability without inclusion
is an incomplete solution.

AI systems are becoming increasingly capable — but the question of who they serve, and where they fail, is still being written. My research asks a specific question: where does that gap show up in the physical world, and what does it take to close it through AI and ML? I approach that question from lived experience as a full-time power wheelchair user with SMA Type 2, grounded in the belief that the people most affected by inaccessible systems are best positioned to design the solutions. My research spans AI and machine learning — from computer vision and on-device inference to responsible system design — with a focus on building technology that works for everyone it touches, not just those it was designed around. Relay is my empirical answer in progress.

IT Knowledge Base Builder

Two-Stage Local LLM Pipeline  ·  Fowler USD

Turns years of resolved help-desk tickets into a searchable internal wiki, running entirely on district-hosted infrastructure so no data leaves the network. A local llama3.1:8b model extracts structured JSON from each ticket; qwen2.5:14b writes the article. Semantic duplicate detection via nomic-embed-text embeddings merges new information into existing articles, and low-confidence extractions route to human review instead of publishing silently. Ran 31 real tickets end to end into 30 generated articles, served through a Flask and SQLite staff portal.

Local LLMsOllama EmbeddingsPython FlaskSQLite
